Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Affiliate Bot ("the app") is a personal content-scheduling tool used only by its owner for the owner's own accounts. This page explains what data the app handles.

1. Data the app receives

  • From TikTok, after the owner logs in: an access token and refresh token, the account's open ID and display name, and the status of content the app uploads.
  • From Threads: an access token and the account's user ID and username.

The app does not collect data about other TikTok or Threads users, followers or viewers.

2. How the data is used

Only to upload and publish the owner's own content to the owner's own accounts, and to check whether an upload succeeded.

3. Where the data is stored

Tokens and account IDs are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are not sent to any server other than the official TikTok and Threads APIs.

Images that will be posted are temporarily placed on a public web page (GitHub Pages) so TikTok can fetch them. These images contain only the post content and are deleted automatically after 14 days.

4. Sharing

The app does not sell, rent or share any data with third parties.

5. Deleting data

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time in TikTok (Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions) or Threads settings, and delete the locally stored tokens.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top of this page shows the latest version.
